## 4. Selected work — aggregate GSC case

**Public framing:** Altus Entertainment (US entertainment platform), authorized by portfolio owner for public attribution on 27/10/2026. Site-wide metrics influenced by multiple contributors and changing site composition. The site and team achieved the trend, framed with a clear transparency disclaimer rather than an isolated personal causal claim.

Periods: Google Search Console, Web search, July 1–September 30, **92 days** each; 2025 and 2026. All reported site-level aggregate, derived from complete daily data. Confidential internal URLs, admin accounts, and raw exports remain strictly private.

| Metric | Q3 2025 | Q3 2026 | Change/reading |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Organic clicks (sitewide) | 13,795 | 17,540 | +3,745 / +27.1% |
| Impressions (sitewide) | 1,358,505 | 1,290,541 | −67,964 / −5.0% |
| CTR (sitewide) | 1.02% | 1.36% | +0.34 percentage points |
| Average position (sitewide) | 28.0 | 23.0 | 5.0 positions better (lower number is better) |
| Non-brand clicks (heuristic) | 13,030 | 17,184 | +4,154 / +31.9% |
| Non-brand CTR (heuristic) | 0.96% | 1.33% | +0.37 percentage points |

Caveats:
- Non-brand excludes queries containing the brand string from website totals; other variants may remain.
- GSC average position is impression-weighted; do not treat it as a literal single-keyword rank.
- A rise in clicks alongside falling impressions does not mean all metrics rose.
- New pages contributed materially to the site's growth; never claim controlled experimental causality.
- Do not describe Q3 2025 as pre-optimization baseline caused by this individual.
