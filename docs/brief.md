# Content and design brief

## Goal

A personal portfolio for SEO Executive Phạm Tiến Đạt / Dat Pham, serving both job applications and potential collaborations. It should show the work through a credible case, published content, career progression, and a clear contact route. The site must be bilingual (VI/EN), have a thoughtful mobile layout, and work without a portrait.

## Voice

Professional, specific, and readable. The user found earlier drafts dry and generic. Explain why the work matters in concrete terms, but do not oversell. Keep the website itself focused on the visitor rather than implementation notes.

## Existing structure

1. Hero with name and SEO positioning.
2. Approach to search intent and content.
3. Aggregate search performance case with selectable metrics and appropriate attribution.
4. Published Primas writing under the author name Dat Pham.
5. Career timeline and SEO capabilities.
6. Contact and LinkedIn.

The current site is an editorial one-page implementation with light/dark sections, large typography, mobile menu, bilingual controls, and motion. Its appearance remains open to revision after proper inspection of the references.
